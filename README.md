
# 📊 Customer Segmentation & Marketing Analytics

An end-to-end Data Analytics project that uses retail transaction data to understand customer purchasing behavior, identify customer segments, analyze revenue contribution, and generate actionable marketing strategies.

The project combines **Python, SQL, RFM Analysis, K-Means Clustering, Estimated Customer Value, Data Visualization, and Streamlit** into an interactive analytics dashboard.

---

## 🎯 Project Objective

Businesses have customers with different purchasing behaviors. Some customers purchase frequently and spend more, while others purchase rarely or have become inactive.

The objective of this project is to:

- Analyze customer purchasing behavior
- Identify valuable customer groups
- Segment customers based on their behavior
- Analyze revenue contribution by segment
- Identify high-value customers
- Identify customers who may be at risk
- Generate targeted marketing recommendations
- Present the results through an interactive dashboard

---

## 💼 Business Problem

A business should not treat every customer in the same way.

For example:

- High-value customers should be retained and rewarded.
- Loyal customers should be encouraged to spend more.
- Potential customers should be converted into loyal customers.
- At-risk customers should receive win-back campaigns.
- Lost customers should be reactivated selectively.

Customer segmentation helps the business make these decisions using data.
